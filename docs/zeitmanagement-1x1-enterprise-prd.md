# PRD: Zeitmanagement 1x1 Premium Edition for Small Business Leaders

## 1. Background & Context
- **Existing Asset**: "Zeitmanagement 1x1" online course currently positioned for students and general audiences on studienstrategie.de.
- **Opportunity**: Repackage and rewrite the course landing page and sales materials to resonate with self-employed professionals, small business owners, and corporate leaders who demand higher-impact outcomes and are willing to pay a premium price.
- **Strategic Goal**: Increase perceived value and course pricing by aligning content with leadership pain points, ROI-driven messaging, and executive-grade credibility cues.

## 2. Problem Statement
Current messaging emphasizes study techniques and individual productivity tips, which underserve decision-makers running teams or companies. Without tailored narratives around strategic time leverage, delegation, and scalable workflows, the course fails to attract higher-paying leadership clientele.

## 3. Objectives & Success Metrics
| Objective | KPI | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elevate brand positioning for leadership audience | Qualitative feedback from 5 target users | 80% report strong resonance |
| Drive premium conversions | Landing page conversion rate | +20% vs. previous student-focused baseline |
| Support higher pricing | Average order value | ≥ €497 per seat |
| Improve lead capture | Download rate for executive checklist lead magnet | 35% visitor-to-lead |

## 4. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Sophie Kramer – Agency Founder (Self-employed)**
   - 10-person marketing agency, €1.5M ARR.
   - Pain: Operates in reactive mode, struggles to prioritize strategic growth.
   - Goal: Free up 10+ hours/week for business development.
2. **Daniel Vogt – SME Managing Director**
   - 45-person manufacturing firm.
   - Pain: Meetings overrun, no time for leadership coaching.
   - Goal: Implement team-wide time discipline and delegation systems.
3. **Aylin Berger – Corporate VP**
   - Leads cross-functional teams in a scale-up.
   - Pain: Multiplies responsibilities across regions; burnout risk.
   - Goal: Embed time-leverage rituals that keep her high-performing.

## 5. User Needs & Pinpoints
- Frameworks that translate into team-level execution (meeting cadences, delegation playbooks).
- Tools for measuring time ROI, aligning focus with quarterly OKRs, and resisting firefighting mode.
- Case studies/examples from founders, SMEs, and executives rather than students.
- Messaging that highlights revenue impact, leadership energy, and culture transformation.
- Certification or completion badge to showcase professional development.

## 6. Key Value Propositions
- **Strategic Time Architecture**: Step-by-step blueprint to design leadership calendars around high-leverage initiatives.
- **Delegation Operating System**: Templates for assigning ownership, feedback loops, and accountability dashboards.
- **Executive Resilience Toolkit**: Stress-management and boundary-setting routines for sustained performance.
- **Scalable Workflow Automation**: Tech stack recommendations tailored to lean teams.
- **Premium Support Layer**: Optional group coaching/Q&A cohorts to reinforce adoption.

## 7. Product Scope
### 7.1 Deliverables
- Rewritten landing page copy (hero, value stack, curriculum highlights, testimonials, FAQs) targeting leadership personas.
- High-ticket pricing table (single seat, team bundle, enterprise license) with urgency triggers.
- Bonus assets: downloadable executive planner, delegation scorecard, ROI calculator.
- Email nurture sequence (5-touch) emphasizing leadership outcomes.
- Updated imagery and iconography aligned with executive aesthetics (muted palette, professional photography).

### 7.2 Out of Scope
- Core course curriculum overhaul beyond messaging.
- New video production (can be scheduled as follow-up project).
- Localization beyond German (retain German-language copy with leadership tone).

## 8. Functional Requirements
1. **Content Strategy**
   - Tone: authoritative yet empathetic, citing business impact and leadership credibility.
   - Structure: Problem → Agitation → Outcome framing, with social proof from founders/executives.
   - CTA: "Jetzt Premium-Zugang sichern" with secondary CTA for team demo.
2. **Pricing & Packaging**
   - Introduce Premium (€497), Team Pack (5 seats, €1,995), Executive Intensive (coaching add-on, €4,995).
   - Include risk-reversal (30-day implementation guarantee).
3. **Lead Capture**
   - Inline form for executive checklist download.
   - Integrate CRM tagging for persona segmentation (self-employed, SME, corporate).
4. **Analytics**
   - Track conversion funnels via Google Analytics 4 + CRM events.
   - A/B testing plan for hero headline and CTA placement.

## 9. Content Requirements & Structure
1. **Hero Section**
   - Headline emphasizing reclaiming strategic bandwidth (e.g., "Führen mit Fokus: In 6 Wochen zu 10 Stunden Mehrwert-Zeit pro Woche").
   - Subheadline referencing leadership outcomes and ROI.
   - CTA buttons (primary purchase, secondary discovery call).
2. **Pain vs. Promise Section**
   - Bullet pain points framed for founders/leaders (scattered teams, firefighting, revenue leakage).
   - Mirror with benefits (strategic clarity, execution rhythm, scalable delegation).
3. **Curriculum Overview**
   - Highlight modules reframed as leadership systems (Strategic Planning Sprint, Delegation Flywheel, High-Performance Rituals).
4. **Evidence & Social Proof**
   - Testimonials from executive clients.
   - Data points (hours saved, revenue unlocked).
5. **Pricing & Guarantee**
   - Tiered packages, guarantee, bonus assets.
6. **FAQ**
   - Address leadership-specific questions (team roll-out, integration with OKRs, time commitment).

## 10. UX & Design Considerations
- Layout optimized for desktop and mobile decision-makers (60% desktop traffic expected).
- Professional, minimalistic visuals with photography of executives in action.
- Infographics illustrating time ROI, delegation cascades, calendar architecture.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance for typography and color contrast.
